Roofing Questions Homeowners Commonly Ask

Answers to common questions about leaks, storm damage, replacements, and what to expect when roof problems show up.

What kinds of roof problems do you handle?

We focus on active leaks, storm damage, missing shingles, flashing failures, aging roof sections, interior water stains, and homeowner concerns about whether the roof can be repaired or should be replaced.

Can you help if I do not know whether I need a repair or replacement?

Yes. Many homeowners are not sure whether a roof problem needs a focused repair or a bigger solution. The first goal is to understand the issue clearly so the right recommendation can follow.

What should I include when I request an estimate?

Include what problem you see, where it is happening, whether the roof leaked after a storm, and how urgent the situation feels. That context helps us understand the likely repair path faster.

Which areas do you serve?

We focus on Wayne and surrounding North Jersey communities, including nearby homeowners looking for roof repair, replacement, storm damage help, or a professional inspection.

What if I am seeing signs of storm damage right now?

If shingles are missing, flashing is bent, or you are seeing new water intrusion after a storm, document what you can safely from the ground and request an estimate as soon as possible.
